Are they running ads? Only on Google, and only for the wholesale shop. The company behind it is CANNUL8 LTD. One ad was last shown today.

Meta keeps every UK ad in its library for 12 months, so "no Meta ads" is a clean result for that window. Older ads (before Nov 2025) are not visible anywhere.

Are they running ads for the courses? No. The course site (kissempire.com) has a Meta pixel but no live ads on Meta, Google or TikTok. Courses sell through Instagram, free lead magnets and email.
So what is the strategy? Three accounts feed each other. The founder builds trust, the wholesale account sells to practitioners, the clinic account sells to patients. Partner clinics pay for their own ads under the Kiss name.

How a practitioner gets sold a course

  1. Instagram post says "Comment the word SIX". Pinned on @kiss_aesthetics_empire since 3 Feb 2026, posted as a collab with Antonia. People still comment "Six" weeks later. The DM sends the join link. See the post
  2. Free lead magnet, name and email. 1-hour workshop, 40+ legal templates, content calendar, 112 reel hooks, ChatGPT prompts, phone camera settings, a "clinic wealth" quiz and an income calculator. The last two also ask for a phone number.
  3. Offer on the thank-you page. 50% off Six Figure Syringe with code 0325 that "disappears forever". A £7 caption pack "was £97" with a countdown. Lip Elite Pro with a timer.
  4. Email and launches. Kajabi email list, a "SFS newsletter", a waitlist page promising an "8pm Monday launch link" and a book-a-call button.
  5. Cross-sell into wholesale. Students get code STUDENT 10 for 10% off their first stock order. A course buyer becomes a filler buyer.
They have done paid ads before, by their own account. Antonia's 2023 podcasts talk about funnels, split tests, a US funnel builder and "100K months" from courses. Her £997 course teaches video ads. Right now they are not running any. Inference They likely pulled back on course ads and now lean on the audience they already built.

The flywheel

  1. Antonia (founder) posts mum life and her story: "My ex called it a stupid little business… ten years later". This builds trust with women in aesthetics.
  2. Wholesale + courses account sells stock and courses to those practitioners. Antonia co-posts here so both audiences see it.
  3. Clinic account sells treatments to patients. Practitioners who buy in can join as "Kiss Associates" under the brand.
  4. Associates pay for local reach. They keep their name, rent their own room, Kiss takes a split. Antonia in 2023: "I've got 30 clinics… I don't own one of them."

What to take from this

  • They are not out-spending anyone. Zero Meta ads in a year. Their edge is ten years of audience, a founder story and a big team on camera.
  • Their reach is weaker than the follower count suggests. 216K followers, a few thousand views per reel. Paid Meta ads would beat them on reach in a local area.
  • Their clinic site can't retarget. No Meta pixel, no Google Ads tag. Anyone running retargeting in the same towns has an easy gap.
  • Worth copying: comment-to-DM on a pinned post, before and afters with exact ml, a voucher that captures email, team members as recurring faces, and a VIP group for repeat bookings.
  • Their course funnel is the most built-out part. Many free lead magnets, a cheap monthly membership, thank-you page offers, then wholesale codes. No ads needed because it rides the Instagram audience.
